# Implementation Plan: 20-Slide Comprehensive Technical Presentation

This plan outlines the expansion of the UAMS presentation from a 10-slide summary to a 20-slide deep dive. The goal is to provide enough detail for a formal DBMS Lab defense, covering every major module and technical achievement.

## Presentation Structure (20 Slides)

1.  **Slide 1: Title Slide** - Project name, team members, institution.
2.  **Slide 2: Introduction & Motivation** - The shift from manual to digital.
3.  **Slide 3: Project Objectives** - Automation, integrity, and transparency.
4.  **Slide 4: System Architecture** - Full-stack diagram (React, Spring Boot, MySQL).
5.  **Slide 5: Technology Stack** - Deep dive into Java 17, JWT, and Vite.
6.  **Slide 6: Database Schema Overview** - The 28-table structure.
7.  **Slide 7: 3NF Normalization** - Step-by-step logic and integrity constraints.
8.  **Slide 8: Admin Module: User Management** - Role-based security and auto-credentials.
9.  **Slide 9: Admin Module: Academic Configuration** - Programs, Batches, and Course mappings.
10. **Slide 10: Registrar Module: Financial Setup** - Fee structures and batch-semester fees.
11. **Slide 11: Registrar Module: Workflow Approvals** - Result validation and document processing.
12. **Slide 12: Faculty Module: Academic Governance** - Advisor approvals and advisee tracking.
13. **Slide 13: Faculty Module: Classroom Operations** - Digital attendance and marks entry.
14. **Slide 14: DBMS Automation: Triggers (Part 1)** - Seat limits and data protection.
15. **Slide 15: DBMS Automation: Triggers (Part 2)** - Auto-status updates (Graduation flow).
16. **Slide 16: DBMS Automation: Stored Procedures** - The CGPA calculation engine (Logic & Cursor).
17. **Slide 17: Student Module: Academic Portal** - Routines, results, and grade sheets.
18. **Slide 18: Student Module: Life & Finance** - Scholarship applications, Ledger, and Services.
19. **Slide 19: Investigation & Results** - Analytical views, pass rates, and collection metrics.
20. **Slide 20: Conclusion & Future Roadmap** - Final summary and next steps.
